## Evidence base (re-verified, 2026-dated)
**Anchor — cloro AI Search Index (Jul 6 2026 State + Jul 16 Edition 01):** Fixed 735-query basket (8 verticals × 6 query types + local), 5 engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini, AI Mode). Citation presence: AI Mode 100%, Perplexity 100%, Copilot 98.6%, ChatGPT 98.4%, **Gemini 32.9%**. Sources per answer: AI Mode 19.5, ChatGPT 13.3, Perplexity 10.6, Copilot 5.1, Gemini 4.4 (13.4 when it cites). Compression Q2→Q3: ChatGPT 21→12, AI Mode 21→10, Perplexity stable ~10, Copilot +0.4, Gemini 4→2.5. UGC/platform share: AI Mode 19%, Perplexity 12%, ChatGPT/Copilot <2%. Top domains: YouTube 2,598, Reddit 771, Google 695. Concentration: AI Mode most concentrated (58% from top-10, all Google-owned); Gemini/Copilot most diverse (~17%). Perplexity citation memory 67% day-over-day vs 34% ChatGPT/Gemini.

**Anchor — MR Research (May 24 2026, "Source Selection Differences"):** 11,500 queries; Vu et al. arXiv:2601.16858. GPT-4o median overlap with Google top-10: 0.0%; Perplexity Sonar Pro: 14.3%; Gemini 2.5 Flash: 8.5%. ChatGPT top-20 sources = 67.3% of all citations (AuthorityTech, 366K citations). ChatGPT Bing-dependent when browsing; Perplexity own crawler; Gemini Google grounding + entity verification. Perplexity cites 0% Wikipedia, 46.7% Reddit in some categories; ChatGPT moderate Wikipedia; Gemini present Wikipedia via grounding. Two fundamentally different citation philosophies.

**Anchor — MERIIN (Jun 14 2026, "559 Citations Logged"):** 25 questions × 3 engines (ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity); 559 citations → 322 domains. 72% cited by one engine only; 28% by more than one; 13 (4%) by all three. Volume: ChatGPT 4.24/answer, Perplexity 7.80, Gemini 10.32 (2.4× range). HHI 0.009 (dispersed). Commercial sources dominate (434/559); UGC/encyclopedia marginal (6+4). Gemini leaned Wikipedia/Reddit more than other two, but overall pattern was commercial-dominated.

**Anchor — SlateHQ (Aug 27 2026, "How to Get Cited"):** 1,000 questions × 4 engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini + AIO); 31,128 citations. Links per answer: Perplexity 19.08, Claude 12.49, AIO 9.42, ChatGPT 3.10, **Gemini 0.07** (only 16/1000 answers had any link). Page overlap: ChatGPT vs Perplexity 0.9%, Perplexity vs Claude 13.6%. Brand overlap: 35-42% across all pairs. 74.1% of cited pages NOT in Google top-10. Reddit: Perplexity 1,051×, AIO 243×, ChatGPT/Claude **zero**. Position 1 → Perplexity 86.9% citation rate; position 9 → 46%. ChatGPT: links on 90.7% of buying questions, 8.1% research. Gemini: links on 1.6% of answers. "Three engines cited 19,906 different pages. 88.7% from one engine alone."

**Anchor — GetMentioned (Apr 22 2026, "Zero Agreement"):** 412,000 citations, 26,000+ domains, ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini over 30 days. 474 domains cited by all three (<2%). ChatGPT ~19K unique (13,854 exclusive); Perplexity ~12K (6,826 exclusive); Gemini ~1,500 (556 exclusive). ChatGPT = 68% brand/homepage, Wikipedia 0.27%, LinkedIn 0.41%. Perplexity = hybrid (Reddit, YouTube, editorial blogs, marketplaces, listicles, news). Gemini = "listicle engine" 36.77% ranking articles; Wikipedia 0.01%, LinkedIn 0.00%. 64% of Gemini top-100 don't appear in other models' top-500. News + Wikipedia combined <2.5%.

**Anchor — Conductor (May 7 2026, "How AI Search Engines Choose Sources"):** 7 engines, Sep 2025–Mar 2026, 1,056 data points. ChatGPT Search = encyclopedic (Wikipedia top for Education every month for 7 months). ChatGPT = Wikipedia + Reddit hybrid. Perplexity = video-anchored (YouTube for Education + Recommendations every month). AIO = video-biased (YouTube 5/7 intents). AI Mode = exploratory, Google properties for Purchase. Gemini = YouTube-anchored every intent. Claude = brand domains + institutional; never YouTube/Reddit/Wikipedia. "ChatGPT and ChatGPT Search are separate products with meaningfully different citation behavior."

**Anchor — MR Index v2 (Aug 4 2026):** 10,661 answer runs, 17,266 domains, 82 days. Reddit 12.6% overall citation rate — but only from 4 engines (Gemini, AI Mode, AIO, Perplexity); ChatGPT/Claude don't cite it at measurable rates. Top-100 domains: 71 cited by all 6 engines; 29 show gaps. Domain authority explains only r²=0.05 of citation behavior. Community/social platforms: widest engine divergence. 44.2% citations from first 30% of page; semantic HTML tables 2.5× more citations (Stacc).

**Anchor — CiteMetrix (May 18 2026) + Resocial (May 11 2026):** Weighted 5-engine source shares: Wikipedia 32%, Reddit 21%, tier-1 editorial 14%, brand-owned 11%. Per-engine (Resocial table): ChatGPT Wikipedia 47.9%/Reddit 6.1%; Perplexity Wikipedia 12.4%/Reddit 46.7%; Gemini Wikipedia 38.1%/YouTube 11.2%; Claude Wikipedia 41.2%/Tier-1 21.7%; AIO Wikipedia 25.3%/Reddit 18.4%/GitHub 12.1%. AIO: only 38% from top-10 (down from 76% year prior). Gemini: brand mention 83.7% (highest), citation link 21.4% (lowest). Copilot: LinkedIn 43.8% of social, Microsoft ecosystem.

## Original angle / contribution
1. The convergence paradox: engines agree on 35-42% of brands but <14% of sources — same companies, different pages. This is the core thesis.
2. The per-engine signature map: a five-column table with the editorial identity, citation density, source preference, and citation-link behavior for each engine — backed by multiple independent studies converging.
3. The citation compression trend: engines getting terser (ChatGPT 21→12, AI Mode 21→10 in one quarter); fewer slots = higher bar.
4. The Gemini paradox: 83.7% brand mention rate but only 33% citation presence and 0.07 links per answer — the mention-but-don't-link engine.
5. The multi-engine investment matrix: which content investments serve which engines (Wikipedia = cross-engine foundation; Reddit = Perplexity/Google AI only; YouTube = Gemini/AIO; editorial authority = ChatGPT/Claude).
6. The "optimize for AI search" obituary: prove with convergent evidence from 5+ independent studies that a single strategy misses 89% of the citation landscape.
