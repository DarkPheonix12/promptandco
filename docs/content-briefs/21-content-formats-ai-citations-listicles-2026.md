## Original angle / contribution
1. "Ranked ≠ self-promotional": the listicle is the most-cited format, but third-party lists hold 80.9% of listicle citations and self-promotional lists collapse on ChatGPT (3.6%). The winning posture is evaluator, not advocate.
2. The format-to-intent matrix: not "lists win" but "format must match intent" — comparison wins ChatGPT (95%), definition wins per-effort, how-to wins AI Mode/Perplexity, ranked lists win commercial.
3. The 1,000–2,000-word reversal: the pillar-page era is over; tightly scoped, single-intent pages beat 5,000-word mega-guides for retrieval.
4. Structure beats prose: 1.5× scannable lift, 44% extracted from top 30%, 120–180-word extractable sections.
5. Definition-page economics: 27% citation rate from 39 average cited words — the cheapest citation asset per word published.
6. Rankings don't proxy citations: 12% overlap (Ahrefs) and 38% top-10 share (AIO) — measurement via citation share, not position.
