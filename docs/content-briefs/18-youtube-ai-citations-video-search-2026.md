3. The timestamp multiplier: 78% multi-citation across chapters; but Google-only. Treat chapters as a Google-surface lever, not universal.
4. The long tail: 7,083 unique videos cited; top 50 = 14%. A niche video can earn 30-60 citations without going viral.
5. YouTube GEO vs YouTube SEO: different goals, different signals. Watch time matters for SEO; transcript accuracy matters for GEO.
