## Original angle / contribution
1. The "third compression" thesis (Sanders): Yellow Pages → Google first page → AI single answer. One sentence that reframes the entire shift.
2. The two-step pattern: AI determines the consideration set (step one), human social proof determines the choice (step two). You must win both, but step one is the gate.
3. The attribution blind spot: 70-80% of buyer journey is untracked. Shortlist forms inside chatbot before any vendor touchpoint. MQL-based measurement reports on the 20% you can see.
4. The review-AI flywheel: reviews feed LLMs → LLMs cite reviews → buyers verify via reviews. 45% say review citations = #1 confidence signal. 10% more reviews → 2% more AI citations.
5. The shrinking shortlist: 3.2 → 2.5 names. Each slot carries more weight. Being unnamed = not in the race.
6. The conversion gap: AI-referred traffic 5.1x higher conversion than Google organic, but the bigger shift is pre-traffic.
