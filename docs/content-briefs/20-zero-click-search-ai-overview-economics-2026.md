## Evidence base (re-verified, 2026-dated)

**Anchor — SparkToro / Rand Fishkin (Jun 8 2026, Similarweb panel):** 68.01% of US Google searches zero-click Jan–Apr 2026 (up from 60.45% in 2024). AI Overviews on 20%+ of searches; near-60% CTR cut when present. Clicks 1X+ fell 9.51pp (−22.9%). AI Mode = 0.34% of searches. Open web: 232 clicks per 1,000 searches. 20%+ of Americans use AI tool 10X+/month.

**Anchor — Pew Research / Similarweb (Mar 2025 browsing panel, cited 2026):** 900 US adults, 68,879 searches, 12,593 with AI summary. 8% click rate with summary vs 15% without (~47% relative). Clicks on cited sources: 1%. 26% of sessions end after AIO page vs 16% without.

**Anchor — Ahrefs (Dec 2025 vs Dec 2023, 300k keywords):** Position-one CTR on AIO keywords fell 0.073 → 0.016. Counterfactual-adjusted: AIO presence cuts position-one CTR 58.0% (up from 34.5% in Apr 2025).

**Anchor — Seer Interactive / Tracy McDonald (Jun 2024–Feb 2026):** 3,119 informational keywords, 42 orgs, 25.1M organic + 1.1M paid impressions. AIO-keyword organic CTR 1.76% → 0.61% (−65%); non-AIO 2.74% → 1.62%. "Leveling off" 1.3% → 2.4% (Dec 2025 → Feb 2026). Cited brands get +120% more organic clicks per impression (Apr 2026).

**Anchor — Agarwal & Sen (SSRN, Apr 2026):** Randomized field experiment, 1,065 US participants, Jan–Feb 2026. AIO presence: organic clicks −38%, zero-click 54% → 72%.

**Anchor — Google I/O 2026 / Similarweb:** AI Mode >1B monthly users; queries >2× per quarter. AI Mode zero-click ~93%. AI Mode referrals 1.6–2.5% vs traditional 17–19%. Semrush: AI Mode sessions avg 4.3 queries.

**Anchor — Mintec (Aug 8 2026):** AI Mode follow-up queries appear in regular GSC performance report (full queries/positions/clicks); generative AI report (Jun 2026) shows only aggregated impressions. "Yes, pricing" at position 1 = grounding without a click.

**Anchor — Google May 6 2026 five updates (seo-kreativ interpretation):** inline links, hover previews, "Subscribed" label, "Explore new angles" article suggestions. UX-level link tweaks; the complete-answer-makes-click-optional mechanic unchanged.

**Anchor — Cite Solutions (Jul 25 2026):** AIO-query zero-click ~83%; AI Mode ~93%. Average AI citation loses half visibility in ~4.5 weeks. AI search traffic converts ~4× better than SEO traffic.

## Original angle / contribution
1. "Two scoreboards" thesis: clicks (classic SERP) and citations (answer layer) now measure different wins; most teams still use one.
2. The triangulated claim: three independent methods (panel 8-vs-15, position-one counterfactual 58%, randomized field −38%) all confirm AIO suppresses clicks — the first article to present all three side by side.
3. The three-zone model (click / hybrid / citation) with keyword-level segmentation rules and what each zone optimizes for.
4. "Cited brands get +120% more organic clicks" — citation is a click amplifier, not the death of clicks; the survivors of zero-click are the cited.
5. The AI Mode measurement blind spot: follow-up queries in the regular GSC report ("yes, pricing"); how to recognize the 93% zero-click surface in your own data.
6. Google's conceded mechanics: the May 6 2026 updates are "consolation prize economics" (Pew's 1% citation-click rate); they improve attribution, not traffic.
7. Recency as lever: 4.5-week citation half-life; freshness beats authority for the cited source.
