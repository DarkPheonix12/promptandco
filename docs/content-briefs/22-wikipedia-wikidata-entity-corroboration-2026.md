## Original angle / contribution
1. "Entity corroboration is the pre-filter": RAG evaluates whether a page's entity exists and is distinct before content quality ever matters. Citations run on the entity, not the page.
2. "The spine, not the solution": Wikipedia is one node in a corroboration chain (site schema → Wikidata QID → Wikipedia → third-party media). The chain — not any single node — produces consensus.
3. "Wikipedia is ChatGPT's spine specifically": the 47.9% top-10 share is a ChatGPT phenomenon (Conductor: only Wikipedia-anchored engine); Perplexity is Reddit-anchored and Wikipedia-inverse. Brand investment must follow the engine your buyers use.
4. "The low-barrier entry is Wikidata": no notability threshold; QID anchor + sameAs gets 3.2× panels and 2.7× AIO citations — the realistic route for brands below Wikipedia's bar.
5. "The concentration risk": 13.15% + 11.97% = 25%+ of ChatGPT citations to two domains; ChatGPT is visibly redistributing (Wikipedia 55%→under 20%) — single-node dependence is fragile; corroboration breadth is the hedge.
6. "Rank to be found, corroborate to be cited": 85% of retrieved pages are never cited — retrieval ≠ citation; corroboration decides the latter.
